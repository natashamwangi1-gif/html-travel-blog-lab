Adventures in Fire Island

My trip highlights

My stay on Fire Island was the ultimate peaceful escape, where the absence of cars made everything slow down beautifully. I spent my days walking along the wooden boardwalks, listening to nothing but the rhythm of the crashing Atlantic waves and the rustle of the sea grass. Reaching the wide, untouched beach felt like stepping into a hidden sanctuary, offering a quiet solitude that made it easy to unwind, disconnect, and completely recharge by the ocean.
